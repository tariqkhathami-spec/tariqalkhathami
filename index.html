Patent Impact

Cited by patent examiners in two countries

“Robots for Servicing Metal Equipment” (US 12,715,108 B2) uses propellers to counteract the robot’s weight while magnetic wheels keep it attached to the metal surface, so a heavier, more capable robot can climb vessel walls, work beneath equipment, and stay on the asset while inspecting or servicing it.

USPTO examiners cited the published application as prior art when examining a Kawasaki Heavy Industries patent (US 12,741,381 B2), and a CNIPA examiner cited it when examining a Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics patent (CN120621525). The patent family is granted in the United States and filed in China, South Korea, and Europe.
